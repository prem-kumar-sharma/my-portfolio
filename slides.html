Introduction & Objectives

Established under the Anusandhan National Research Foundation Act, 2023, the PAIR initiative is designed to strengthen India’s research and innovation ecosystem.

It utilizes a multi-institutional Hub-and-Spoke model focusing on mentorship and shared research. The primary aims are to foster collaboration, enhance capacity building, and drive translational innovation.
