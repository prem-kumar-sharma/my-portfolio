(01) About

I'm an engineer and researcher working where research meets production. At IIIT Hyderabad's Precog lab, with Prof. Ponnurangam Kumaraguru, I build grounded, production-ready AI systems. Before that, I researched deepfake detection at IIT Bombay and shipped GenAI at LOQO AI.

Currently a Research Associate (AI Engineer) at Precog, IIIT Hyderabad, working with Prof. Ponnurangam Kumaraguru (PK), where I build grounded, production-ready AI systems for national-scale initiatives.

Before that, I spent a year at IIT Bombay under Prof. Vikram M. Gadre, researching deepfake detection and minutiae matching in contact-based and contactless fingerprints with wavelets and time-frequency methods, and coordinating the ANRF-PAIR national research program.

Earlier, as a Generative AI Engineer at LOQO AI, I architected multi-agent pipelines and text-to-video systems with GPT-4/5, Gemini, LLaMA, Stable Diffusion and ElevenLabs, and applied RAG and fine-tuning to ship production GenAI.

I also mentor students in Python, AI, careers and computational thinking, and share the real process of figuring it out with 18K+ people on YouTube and LinkedIn. No polished narratives.
